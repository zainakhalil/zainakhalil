As a kid in Cairo, I’d sit with my grandparents while their stories slipped between Arabic and English mid-sentence. That’s where my questions about language began: how do we move between languages, and what does that reveal about how we think?

Today, I’m studying for my **Master of Computer Science at UIUC** and exploring a new version of that question: **what happens when multilingual learners learn with AI?** My work brings together language, learning sciences, human-computer interaction, and data.
